# Public Knowledge Platform decision record

The owner decided the five questions below. The source decision record is node_01KZ755YBS3A99PVJBC53R4DTY; the [PRD](../PRDs/features/public-knowledge-platform-v1.md#8-decided-design-decisions) records their consequences and considered alternatives. These decisions do not approve implementation or launch.

| Decision | Chosen option | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D-open-1 | B — Research flows through the toolkit while direct open access remains. | The toolkit can connect library use and contribution while preserving direct reads and searches beyond the library. |
| D-open-2 | D — Short claim quotes and a per-source cap form a common floor, with source-license exceptions. | This aims to preserve useful public claims while accounting for source-specific permissions. |
| D-open-3 | C — Seed a small mix of agent-tools/software-engineering and research-methods domains. | This tests reuse across complementary domains with bounded seed scope. |
| D-open-4 | C — Separate now, connect later. | A separate public library protects private data at launch and leaves room for users to connect a local instance to the public library and other libraries later while retaining a private local library. |
| D-open-5 | A — Compare models on seed sources, then publish pass rates by declared model. | Comparable seed results guide model selection; reported contribution outcomes provide further evidence without verifying model identity. |

The owner owns a separate future legal review as an open launch prerequisite. It must assess the proposed floor, cap and license exceptions before public launch; it has not been commissioned or completed by this decision.
