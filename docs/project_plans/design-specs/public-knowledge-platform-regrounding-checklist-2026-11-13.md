# Re-grounding Checklist

The 2026-07-26 baseline is compared with this checkout, not with the superseded July hand-off. The shipped activation PRD and commits `99ef69d` and `08559a0` are the release record. Its historical baseline paragraphs describe pre-activation state, not the resulting implementation. “Unmeasured” means repository evidence cannot establish the live deployment or an exhaustive negative. File pointers are repository-relative and refer to the checkout at review time.

Verdicts: **verified** (claim supported within stated scope), **changed** (material qualification or later change), **absent** (claimed capability absent in the inspected surface), **unmeasured** (insufficient evidence). Source-level inspection does not certify a deployed instance.
