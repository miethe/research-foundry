Research Foundry Public Release Program Status

Research Foundry is already public as source and documentation, and the evidence, assertion, catalog, rights, and trusted-cohort multi-user substrates are substantial. Its differentiated market value is not another literature-search UI: it is accumulated, reviewable assertion capital with source passage, version, lifecycle, evaluation, rights, and downstream-use provenance. The current application remains LAN-hosted rather than a revision-proven public service. Recommended release sequence: formal OSS packaging, curated read-only showcase, invited beta, private ingestion, reviewed public contribution, then federation after interchange, promotion, shared-index, and tombstone contracts mature.
